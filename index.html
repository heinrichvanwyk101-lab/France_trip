LEG 2

Disneyland Paris

18–19 Jul · 2 parks

On-site hotel, full-board meal plan and Extra Magic Time, no Premier Access. With no fast-track, early starts and Extra Magic Time carry the day. Do-now: book restaurant tables in the app (the meal plan doesn’t reserve them) — character meals sell out months ahead.

Sat 18 JulDisneyland Park · the castle park

  • Extra Magic Time: Peter Pan’s Flight first, then Fantasyland — it’s a small world, Dumbo, Carousel, Snow White.
  • Late morning: Big Thunder Mountain (if Alexander is 1.02 m+), Pirates, Buzz Lightyear. Use Rider Switch on anything he can’t ride.
  • 13:00 heat-block lunch: Blue Lagoon (inside Pirates) — cool, magical, air-conditioned.
  • 14:00–18:00: hotel rest, or indoor rides (Pirates, Phantom Manor, small world).
  • Evening: parade, castle at dusk, nighttime show — ear defenders ready.

Sun 19 JulDisney Adventure World

  • Extra Magic Time: straight to World of Frozen — claim the free Frozen Ever After virtual queue, then Raiponce Tangled Spin; meet Anna / Elsa / Olaf.
  • Late morning: Ratatouille, Cars Road Trip, Spider-Man W.E.B., Slinky Dog, Toy Story Playland.
  • 13:00 heat-block lunch: Bistrot Chez Rémy (Ratatouille) — book early.
  • 14:00–18:00: hotel rest or indoor; pack — you move to the Paris base after.
  • Late afternoon: last favourites, then transfer to Normandy Le Chantier.

Standby playbook & the character meal

  • Free  Frozen Ever After virtual queue — claim in the app the moment you enter each morning.
  • Free  Rider Switch at every height-restricted ride — one adult waits with Alexander, then swaps without re-queuing.
  • Free  Single Rider lines (Crush’s Coaster, Hyperspace Mountain, Spider-Man W.E.B., RC Racer) cut adult waits.
  • Height: most rides no minimum; Big Thunder / Tower of Terror / Star Tours need 1.02 m; Crush’s Coaster 1.07 m. Measure Alexander in his park shoes.
  • One character meal is plenty: Regal View (new, Day 2) or Auberge de Cendrillon (Day 1). Book the instant you can — it saves the Princess Pavilion queue.
  • Note: Plaza Gardens and Annette’s Diner are closed for renovation — ignore older guides that list them.
