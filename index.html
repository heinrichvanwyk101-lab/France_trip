Breakfast: sorted at Disney, Normandy and the Meliá (Barcelona). The two Paris bookend hotels charge extra — €14 pp/night at the Eiffel Petit Louvre (one arrival morning) and €22 pp/night at Charles V (≈€110/day for five, ~€330 over three mornings), so plan to eat out. City tax is paid at each property (Paris €8.45 pp/night, Meliá €9.24/adult/night, under-17s exempt); the Paris hotels also take a card deposit on arrival (€100 Eiffel Petit Louvre, €200 Normandy).

Transfers to lock in: pre-booked van at CDG both evenings (5 + luggage); Beauvais→Marais private minivan ~€200 on 28 Jul; Narbonne↔Homps taxis on 20 & 24 Jul — the 24th early taxi is the trip’s tightest link. Paris métro is mostly not step-free — route the grandparents by bus / RER / taxi.

LEG 1

Disneyland Paris

18–19 Jul · 2 parks

On-site hotel with Extra Magic Time (early entry, ~08:30 — check the app the night before) and full board; no Premier Access. The play: rope-drop the headliners during Extra Magic Time, then work down by rank. Alexander (1.2 m) is tall enough for everything here bar the most intense coasters. Do-now: book restaurant tables in the app — the meal plan doesn’t reserve them, and character meals sell out months ahead.

How to win the morning

  • Key  No sit-down breakfast first. Be at the gates as Extra Magic Time opens, ride the first 2–3 headliners while queues are minimal, then breakfast ~10:00 as the crowds build — usually saves an hour+ of queueing.
  • Free  Frozen Ever After virtual queue — claim it in the app the instant you enter on Day 2.
  • Free  Rider Switch at any height ride — one adult waits with Alexander, then swaps without re-queuing.
  • Free  Single Rider (Crush’s Coaster, Hyperspace Mountain, Spider-Man W.E.B., RC Racer) cuts adult waits.
